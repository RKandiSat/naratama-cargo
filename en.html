SERVICES

Three modes, one team handling all of them.

You do not need a separate vendor for each leg. We connect them.

Land Freight

Intercity and inter-island delivery by road and ferry, for full or consolidated loads.

  • FTL — a dedicated vehicle for your cargo alone
  • LTL — consolidated loads, pay for the space you use
  • Pickup at warehouse, factory or residence
  • Delivery to the destination address, not just to a terminal

Sea Freight

The most economical option for large, heavy shipments, domestic and international.

  • FCL — a full container (20ft / 40ft / 40HC)
  • LCL — consolidated cargo sharing a container
  • Shipping documentation and Bill of Lading handling
  • Loading and discharge coordination at both ports

Air Freight

For urgent, high-value or perishable cargo.

  • Fast delivery between major cities, usually next-day arrival
  • Suited to spare parts, critical documents and perishables
  • Air Waybill handling and cargo security screening
  • Pickup and delivery to and from the airport

DOCUMENTS & CUSTOMS

The paperwork we handle, so your cargo is not held up.

Most cargo delays are not caused by the vehicle but by one missing document. This is what we prepare and check before anything moves.

Delivery order & packing list

Contents, number of packages, weight and dimensions of each unit.

Bill of Lading (B/L)

The sea transport document and title to the goods.

Air Waybill (AWB)

The air transport document, which also serves as the tracking number.

Export & import declarations

Customs declarations for cross-border shipments.

HS code classification

Determining the commodity code that sets the duty and permits that apply.

Cargo insurance

Cover for the value of the goods while in transit, on request.

FAQ

The questions we get most.

How soon will I get a quote?

Within one working day for routes we run regularly. For destinations we need to check with partners first it is usually two working days, and we tell you if it will take longer.

What do you need from me to quote?

Origin and destination, the commodity, weight and volume or dimensions per package, and your ready date. For cross-border shipments, add the incoterm and the cargo value.

Can you collect cargo outside Bali?

Yes. We are based in Badung, but we arrange pickup in Java, Lombok and other major cities through our partner network.

What if cargo is damaged or lost?

A carrier’s liability is set by the transport document and is capped. For high-value goods we recommend cargo insurance, and we arrange it before the shipment moves.

Can you handle dangerous goods or temperature-controlled cargo?

Ask us first and name the commodity. Both have their own rules and limits per mode, and we will say plainly if we cannot take it.

Is there a minimum shipment size?

There is no minimum for consolidated cargo. FTL and FCL are priced per vehicle or per container, so they make sense from a certain volume up — we will tell you honestly which is cheaper for you.
